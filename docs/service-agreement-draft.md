## 2. Term
The initial term is 12 months, starting on the date the Site goes live ("Launch Date"). After that,
this Agreement renews month to month until either party cancels with 30 days' written notice.

## 3. Fees and payment
(a) The setup or build fee is due on signing and is non-refundable once work has begun.
(b) Monthly fees start on the Launch Date and are charged automatically by card or ACH.
(c) **Early cancellation:** if Client cancels during the initial term, Client pays the lesser of the
remaining monthly fees in the initial term or $300.
(d) **Late payment:** if a payment is 30 days overdue, Provider may pause the Site (replace it with a
notice page) until the account is current. Provider will email Client at least 7 days before pausing.
(e) Provider may change fees after the initial term with 60 days' written notice.

## 4. Edits
(a) An "Edit" is a change to existing Site content: text, service times, events, staff, photos, links
or adding a special service. New pages, new features, redesigns and content migrations are not Edits
and are quoted separately.
(b) Included Edits per month: Essentials, 1 small Edit; Ministry, up to 1 hour; Custom, up to 3 hours.
Unused time does not roll over.
(c) Turnaround: Edits are completed within 3 business days of a complete request (Custom: 1 to 2).
(d) Additional time is billed at $60/hour. Provider will get Client's approval before doing billable work.
(e) Requests are sent to [edits email].

## 5. Client responsibilities
Client will provide content, photos, logo and approvals promptly, and confirms it has the right to use
everything it provides (including photos of people and music or video it embeds). Client is
responsible for the accuracy of its content and for its third-party accounts (giving provider,
YouTube, Google Calendar, domain registrar).

## 6. Ownership
(a) Client owns its content, logo, photos and domain name.
(b) Provider owns its shared templates, components and code ("Platform"), and grants Client a license
to use them for the Site during this Agreement. Custom-tier design work created specifically for
Client is assigned to Client upon full payment of the build fee.
(c) **On exit:** when this Agreement ends and all fees are paid, Provider will, within 14 days, give
Client a full export of the Site (static files and content) and help transfer the domain.

## 7. Review and launch
Client receives a preview and one round of revisions (two for Custom) before launch. Further revisions
before launch are billed as additional time.

## 8. Availability
Provider uses a reputable hosting provider and will use reasonable efforts to keep the Site available,
but does not guarantee uninterrupted service. Provider will restore from backup at no charge if the Site
is lost or damaged through no fault of Client.

## 9. Limitation of liability
Provider's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees Client paid in the 3 months
before the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, including lost
donations or lost data held by third-party services.
